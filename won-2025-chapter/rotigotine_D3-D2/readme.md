# D3R versus D2R (rotigotine)

Distance differences D3R - D2R: 8IRT minus 8IRS, both active and bound to rotigotine.

 - **Green (+1):** the distance is larger in D3R.
 - **Brown (-1):** the distance is larger in D2R.
 - **White (0):** the difference does not exceed the threshold T.
 - **Lower-left triangle:** side-chain center of mass (COM) distances. **Upper-right triangle:** backbone distances.

The chapter prints four of these panels: the sub-segments at T = 0.0 and 0.6 Å (Fig. 13.3) and the binding site residues at T = 0.0 and 0.6 Å (Fig. 13.4). Each is marked below. The other thresholds and the extracellular-end panels are additional results from the same run.

The binding site residues also have an **orthosteric binding site (OBS)** panel, the same matrix cut to the 12 OBS residues: 3.32, 3.33, 3.36, EL2.52, 5.42, 5.46, 6.48, 6.51, 6.52, 6.55, 7.39, 7.43.
