## Citation
Won SJ, Lee KH, Shi L. [Unveiling activation mechanisms and intrinsic divergence of dopamine receptors based on experimentally determined structures](https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-443-29867-7.00022-0). In: *The Handbook of Dopamine* (Handbook of Behavioral Neuroscience). Elsevier; 2025. p. 155-169. doi: 10.1016/B978-0-443-29867-7.00022-0

## Scope
A separate analysis from the two papers above. The activation-related conformational changes cover the D2-like receptors DRD2, DRD3 and DRD4, with the structures listed in Fig. 13.2 of the chapter, so the DRD2 and DRD3 panels here draw on more structures than those in [lee-2023-pmid-37540602](../lee-2023-pmid-37540602/README.md) and differ from them accordingly. The intrinsic divergence compares two pairs of receptors bound to the same agonist, rotigotine: D3R with D2R, and D5R with D1R (Figs. 13.3 and 13.4).
