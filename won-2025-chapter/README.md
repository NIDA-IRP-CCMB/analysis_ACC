## Citation
Won SJ, Lee KH, Shi L. [Unveiling activation mechanisms and intrinsic divergence of dopamine receptors based on experimentally determined structures](https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-443-29867-7.00022-0). In: *The Handbook of Dopamine* (Handbook of Behavioral Neuroscience). Elsevier; 2025. p. 155-169. doi: 10.1016/B978-0-443-29867-7.00022-0

## Data source
A separate analysis from the two papers above, using the 2024-01-29 and 2024-02-22 releases of [GPCRdb](https://gpcrdb.org/pages/releasenotes/). Coverage extends to DRD1, DRD4 and DRD5, so the DRD2 and DRD3 panels here draw on more structures than those in [lee-2023-pmid-37540602](../lee-2023-pmid-37540602/README.md) and differ from them accordingly.
