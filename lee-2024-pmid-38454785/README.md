## Data source
The activation-related analyses draw on the aminergic receptor structures selected in [lee-2023-pmid-37540602](../lee-2023-pmid-37540602/README.md); the panels shown here are the dopaminergic subset of that work. The intrinsic divergence analysis compares D3R and D2R structures against each other rather than an active/inactive pair of one receptor.
